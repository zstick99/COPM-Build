* CHOICE CONTENTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Whoa, dudes and dude-ttes! Where do you think you're going. At least see what's inside:

1.) MarioXX.pac/.pcs - The costume files! It's where all the magic happens, big time. For all you modders, I don't need to explain...but for you newbies? It contains everything about a character the game needs to load! All those bodacious models, textures, shaders, etc.? It's all in here.

Some mods are extra righteous, and only need the .pac file. Make sure you check your files beforehand, though!

2.) Cosmetics - All your visual needs are here, right in this folder! CSPs, Battle Portraits, Stocks...everything you need, this tubular folder has it. Dolphin is especially down with modern tech advancements, as it can use HD cosmetics.

Luckily, you can count on old SirGimmick to come in clutch. I've got both SD and HD cosmetics avaliable at your choosing.
